# quarrel — Big Ideas

The strategic altitude: the few capabilities quarrel is being built toward. Each Big Idea is a file
in `BigIdeas/` with a north star, a "done means", its outcome spine and its member ADRs, SOWs and
backlog items. Execution lives in `Outcomes/` (SOWs) and `Backlog/`; backlog items join back through
their `big_idea: BI-NN` field.

**State:** `on` (actively being delivered, has live SOWs) · `simmering` (direction set, next in
line) · `sleeping` (captured, waiting for its wake trigger) · `done`.

Capture freely. A Big Idea can sleep indefinitely; waking it means cutting its first SOW.
